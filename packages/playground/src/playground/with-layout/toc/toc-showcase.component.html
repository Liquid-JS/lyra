Installation

Add the packages with your package manager, then import the theme styles once in your global stylesheet.

The schematic sets up the styles, the theme module and the icons for you.

Configuration

Lyra is configured with providers in the application config.

Themes

Pick one of the built-in themes, or register your own with its own colours and fonts.

Themes switch at runtime, so users can choose a dark theme.

Icons

Register an icon pack, or use the essential icons that the components need.

Usage

Import the components you need into your standalone components.

Every component works by keyboard, and its styles come from the theme.

Accessibility

Controls are labelled, landmarks are named, and focus is visible in every theme.

Questions

Ask in the discussions, or open an issue with a reproduction.
