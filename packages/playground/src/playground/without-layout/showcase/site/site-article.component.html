Every month, Fabrikam's operations team spent a week closing the books. Invoices lived in one tool, customer notes in another, and the numbers that tied them together in three spreadsheets that only one person fully understood.

One place for every customer

The team started by importing their customers from the main spreadsheet. Within a day, every invoice and note sat on the customer it belonged to, and anyone could see where a deal stood without asking around.

Month-end used to be the week we dreaded. Now it's an afternoon, and we actually trust the numbers.

Emma Brown, operations lead

Reports instead of spreadsheets

With invoices and payments in one place, the revenue dashboard updated itself. The team replaced their monthly report with a link, and spent the time they saved talking to customers instead.

  • Closing the books went from five days to half a day.
  • Overdue invoices fell by a third with automatic reminders.
  • Every customer's history is one click away for the whole team.
