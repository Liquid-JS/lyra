Mark complete

Documentation is the part of a design system most teams leave for last, and the part people use most. This lesson builds a component's page from scratch, in the order readers need it: when to use it, how it's put together, and only then what it can do.

By the end you'll have a page template you can apply to every component, and a short checklist for reviewing the pages others write.
